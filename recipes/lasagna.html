Lasagna

Lasagna is a wide, flat or ribbon-shaped sheet of pasta, as well as the popular baked Italian dish made by layering those pasta sheets with sauces, cheeses, and meats or vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Meat Sauce (Ragu)
  • Cheese Layer
  • White Sauce (Bechamel)

Steps

  1. Prep the Meat Sauce Brown the meat: Cook 1 lb ground beef (or sausage) with the diced onion and garlic in a large skillet over medium-high heat until fully browned. Drain excess grease. Simmer: Stir in the tomato paste, crushed tomatoes (or marinara sauce), oregano, basil, salt, and pepper. Lower the heat and simmer for 15 minutes to let the flavors blend.
  2. Mix the Cheese Layer Combine: In a medium bowl, mix the ricotta cheese, the beaten egg, and half of the Parmesan cheese. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper.
  3. Cook the Noodles (If not using oven-ready) Boil: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook 12 lasagna noodles according to package instructions (usually 10 minutes). Drain and rinse with cold water to stop cooking and prevent sticking.
  4. Assemble the Lasagna Base: Spread 1 cup of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish. Layer 1: Place 4 noodles over the sauce. Spread 1/3 of the ricotta mixture, then sprinkle 1/3 of the mozzarella cheese, and top with 1/3 of the remaining meat sauce. Repeat: Repeat the layers (noodles, ricotta, mozzarella, sauce) two more times. Top: For the final top layer, add the last noodles, the remaining meat sauce, the rest of the mozzarella, and the leftover Parmesan cheese.
  5. Bake Cover: Tent the dish with aluminum foil (so it doesn't touch the cheese). Bake: Bake in a preheated oven at 375F (190C) for 25 minutes. Brown: Remove the foil and bake uncovered for another 15 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown. Rest: Let it rest for 15 minutes before slicing so it holds its shape.
