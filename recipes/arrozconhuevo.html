Arroz con huevo

Rice with egg is a traditional, simple, and budget-friendly dish popular across Latin America and other parts of the world. It consists of a serving of cooked white rice topped with one or more eggs.

Ingredients

  • White rice
  • Eggs
  • Cooking oil or butter
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Warm or Cook the Rice If using leftovers (recommended): Reheat 1 cup of cooked white rice in a pan or microwave until steaming hot. If cooking from scratch: Rinse 1 cup of rice. Bring 2 cups of water with a pinch of salt and a splash of oil to a boil. Add the rice, reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes until fluffy.
  2. Fry the Egg Heat 1 tablespoon of oil or butter in a non-stick skillet over medium heat. Crack 1 or 2 eggs gently into the pan. Season with a pinch of salt. Cook for 3 minutes until the whites are fully set but the yolk remains runny and soft.
  3. Assemble and Serve Scoop the hot rice onto a plate. Slide the fried egg right on top of the rice. Optional: Toss in your favorite extras like fried plantains, avocado slices, or a spoonful of hogao.
